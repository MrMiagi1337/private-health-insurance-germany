Frequently asked questions

Who can choose private health insurance in Germany?

Employees who earn above the income threshold, self-employed people and freelancers, and civil servants can usually choose private cover. Students can choose a student tariff. The Eligibility Check shows which route applies to you.

How are private health insurance premiums calculated?

Your premium is set by your age at entry, your health and the level of cover you choose. It does not rise with your salary, which is the main difference from public health insurance.

Do I need to speak German?

No. Your consultant works in English, and several insurers offer full English-language service.

How long does it take to get covered?

Policies typically start within 2 to 4 weeks of the application. Your consultant handles the paperwork with you.

What does the advice cost?

Nothing. The consultation and the comparison are free of charge and without obligation.

Can I go back to public health insurance later?

Only in limited cases, for example if your income drops below the threshold for an extended period. Think carefully before you switch and talk to an independent consultant first.
