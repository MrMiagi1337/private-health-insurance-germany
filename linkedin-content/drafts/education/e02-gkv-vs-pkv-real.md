## Post

GKV vs. PKV in Germany.

Every comparison site gives you the same table. None of them explain what actually matters.

Here's what they leave out:

**GKV (public) — what they don't emphasize:**
→ Your premium is a % of salary. It rises every time you get a raise.
→ If you have a non-working partner or kids: they're insured for free. This is a huge advantage if you have a family.
→ If you become unemployed, the Jobcenter covers your GKV contributions. PKV doesn't work that way.

**PKV (private) — what they don't emphasize:**
→ Your premium is locked in based on your age and health at sign-up. Get in young and healthy, pay less for decades.
→ Premiums DO increase over time — it's a known issue and worth modeling out.
→ If you're self-employed or a freelancer, PKV is often the only sensible option above a certain income.
→ The treatment quality gap between GKV and PKV at specialist level is real and documented — not just marketing.

**The honest conclusion:**
For a single, healthy engineer earning above €77,400: PKV is often financially better and medically better.
For someone with kids, a non-working spouse, or uncertain income: GKV's solidarity model is genuinely valuable.

The answer depends on your life situation. Not on a traffic-light comparison table.
