## Post

€100,000 gross salary in Germany.

Here's what you actually take home — and where the rest goes.

Rough breakdown (single, no church tax, GKV):

→ Income tax: ~€26,000
→ Pension insurance: ~€9,300
→ Health insurance (GKV): ~€6,600
→ Unemployment insurance: ~€1,200
→ Long-term care insurance: ~€1,500

**Net: approximately €55,000 — or €4,600/month**

That's about 45% gone before you touch it.

The part most people miss: once you earn above ~€77,400/year, you can switch to private health insurance.

For a young, healthy professional, a good PKV policy often costs €300–450/month, compared to ~€550/month in GKV contributions.

That's potentially €1,200–3,000/year in savings. With better coverage and faster specialist access on top.

We talk to engineers every week who spent more time optimizing their cloud infrastructure than their own salary situation.

The German system rewards people who read the manual.
