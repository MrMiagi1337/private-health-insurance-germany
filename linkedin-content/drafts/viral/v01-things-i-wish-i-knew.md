## Post

6 things nobody tells you before moving to Germany as a software engineer.

We've helped hundreds of expats navigate the German system. These are the things that catch almost everyone off guard.

→ Your gross salary and your net salary are two very different numbers. €80k sounds great until you see the payslip.

→ The bureaucracy isn't a meme. It's a full-time job for your first 3 months. Block the calendar.

→ "Free" healthcare isn't free. Around 17% of your salary goes to public health insurance, split with your employer. Do the math on your actual salary.

→ Once you earn above ~€77,400/year, you get a choice most countries don't give you: public or private insurance. Most expats don't even know this option exists.

→ Finding an English-speaking doctor you actually trust takes months, not days.

→ The system rewards people who understand it. Most expats don't — and they pay for that for years.

The health insurance decision in particular: almost everyone we talk to rushed it in week one and spent years wondering if they made the right call.
