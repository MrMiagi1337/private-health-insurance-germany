## Post

Moving from the US to Germany?

The healthcare difference will surprise you — but probably not in the direction you expect.

In the US, a typical employer plan costs $500–700/month with a $3,000+ deductible. Every specialist visit is a negotiation. Every MRI comes with a bill you may need to fight.

In Germany's public system (GKV), contributions come out of your paycheck automatically. No bills. No surprise invoices. A lot of expats from the US call it a revelation.

Then they need an MRI.

Public system: 5-week wait for a referral, then another 3 weeks for the scan.
Private patients at the same facility: available the following week.

Germany doesn't have "one" healthcare system. It has two lanes.

And depending on your income level, you actually get to choose which one you're in.

The US system has problems Germany's doesn't. But Germany's system is more complex than most expats realize — and the gap between the two lanes is wider than anyone publicly admits.

The expats who navigate Germany best are the ones who treat learning the system like a project. Especially the healthcare decision.
