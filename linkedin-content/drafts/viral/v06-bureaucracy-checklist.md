## Post

Moving to Germany? Here's the checklist for your first 30 days.

Save this. Nobody hands it to you.

**Week 1**
→ Register your address (Anmeldung) — everything else depends on this
→ Open a German bank account (N26, DKB, or Commerzbank work well for new arrivals)
→ Get a SIM card with a German number

**Week 2**
→ Apply for your tax ID (Steueridentifikationsnummer) — arrives by post in 2–3 weeks
→ Sort out health insurance — this is time-sensitive, don't leave it for month 2
→ If you earn above €77,400/year: don't just sign GKV on autopilot. You have a choice. Compare your PKV options before you commit — switching back later is complicated.

**Week 3–4**
→ Register with a GP (Hausarzt) — wait lists are real, start early
→ Get liability insurance (Haftpflichtversicherung) — cheap, important, 10 minutes online
→ Open a savings account or investment depot if you're planning to stay long-term

The health insurance step is the one most people rush and later question.

20 minutes to sign up. Sometimes years to understand if it was the right call.
