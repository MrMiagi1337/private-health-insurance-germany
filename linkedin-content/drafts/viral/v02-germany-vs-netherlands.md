## Post

Germany vs. Netherlands for software engineers.

We get this question constantly. Here's the honest breakdown.

Netherlands wins on:
→ English everywhere — no German required to survive day-to-day
→ Expat community is massive and well-organized
→ Amsterdam lifestyle is genuinely hard to beat

Germany wins on:
→ Salary ceilings are higher at senior levels
→ Job market depth — especially in fintech, automotive, and enterprise software
→ Lower cost of living outside Munich and Frankfurt
→ Once you earn above the PKV threshold, you get healthcare options the Netherlands simply doesn't offer

That last point surprises most people.

In Germany, above ~€77,400/year you can opt out of the public system and get private health insurance. Faster specialist access, better diagnostic coverage, often cheaper long-term for healthy professionals.

In the Netherlands, everyone pays into the same public system. No opting out.

Neither country is objectively better for everyone. But Germany consistently rewards people who take the time to learn its rules — and the healthcare system is one of the clearest examples of that.
